add-on\ (optional)
==================

This folder is for extra content: more cars (Dodge, Ferrari, Hyundai), the paid edition's extra licence
tests and its 44 kHz audio.  IMPORTANT.TXT next to gtracing.exe says where to find out more.

Each add-on is a folder of its own inside this one, for example:
    add-on\hyundai\...
    add-on\premium-audio\...

The game reads them at the next start.  To turn an add-on off, delete its folder or move it out of add-on\.
A save that holds an add-on car still loads without that add-on: the car is hidden until the folder is back.

The game never reads this note.
